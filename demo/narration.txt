Introducing ArcSwarm, an autonomous multi-agent USDC treasury management system built on Arc.

ArcSwarm uses six specialized AI agents that work together to manage your treasury: a Yield Agent, a Liquidity Agent, an FX Agent, a Payment Agent, a Risk Agent, and a Coordinator Agent.

These agents communicate with each other using Nanopayments on the Arc network, enabling autonomous collaboration.

Let's look at the dashboard. On the left, you can see the sidebar with navigation tabs for Treasury, Agents, Risk Monitor, and Yield Strategies.

The Treasury Overview shows our vault balance of nine USDC, total deposits, yield earned, and the number of active depositors.

The Agent Registry displays all six registered agents on the Arc testnet, showing their names, roles, status, reputation scores, and wallet addresses.

The Risk Monitor panel shows the current risk score of one hundred, health status, and total portfolio exposure. The circuit breaker is active.

The Yield Strategies panel displays yield farming strategies, harvest history, and pending rewards.

Each agent has its own isolated wallet with spending policies managed by the AgentBudgetManager contract.

All transactions are recorded on-chain for full transparency. You can view them on the Arc testnet explorer.

ArcSwarm integrates with Circle App Kits for unified balance, swap, and send operations. It uses the Agent Stack for coordinated agent execution.

The system is fully tested with four hundred and sixty-three tests passing across Solidity contracts, API endpoints, agent logic, and the dashboard.

Built with Foundry, Node.js, TypeScript, React, and Tailwind CSS. Deployed on Arc testnet with USDC as the native gas token.
